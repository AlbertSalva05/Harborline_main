The platform

Four jobs, one board

Everything a dispatcher touches between a posting and a signed rate confirmation, without a second tab.

01Coverage

Load triage board

Every board posting lands in one queue, deduped and priced against your last 90 days on the lane instead of a national average.

02Compliance

Carrier vetting before you confirm

Authority, insurance certificates, and CSA basics pulled live. Expired coverage blocks the rate confirmation instead of warning you afterwards.

03Paperwork

Rate confirmations that close

Send, e-sign, and file a rate con from the load card. The signed copy attaches to the load and the carrier packet at once.

04Margin

Detention you can invoice

Arrival and departure stamps become accessorial line items automatically, so detention you used to write off reaches the invoice.

Carrier vetting

Lapsed insurance blocks the load, not your Friday

Authority, certificates, and CSA basics are checked against FMCSA data while the carrier is still on the phone. If coverage expires before delivery, Harborline stops the rate confirmation instead of telling you afterwards.
